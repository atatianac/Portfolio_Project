**Research question:**
Which academic program areas at California public universities show the greatest mismatch between graduate output and projected regional workforce demand, and where might additional program capacity be most justified?

**Optimization problem:**
Given limited resources for program expansion, how could a university prioritize additional capacity across programs to better respond to projected workforce demand?


**Stakeholder:**
An institutional research or strategic planning team at a California public university or university system.

**Decision:**
They need to decide which academic programs should be considered for expansion when allocating limited faculty, funding, facilities, and enrollment capacity.

**Information needed:**
To make that decision, they need to understand current graduate output by academic program, the occupations those programs commonly prepare students for, current and projected regional labor demand for those occupations, and where projected workforce demand appears high relative to the supply of relevant graduates.

**Primary outcome:**
The analysis should identify academic programs and university-region combinations where additional program capacity may be most justified based on projected workforce demand, current graduate supply, wages, and other labor-market indicators.
